Data source: Adaptyv Foundry experiment operations.

Adaptyv Foundry manages wet-lab experiments from preparation through execution and analysis.
Each experiment has an identifier, organization code, lifecycle status, result-availability
status, experiment type, URL, and detailed experiment specification.

Experiment lifecycle statuses include Draft, WaitingForConfirmation, QuoteSent,
WaitingForMaterials, InQueue, InProduction, DataAnalysis, InReview, Done, and Canceled.
`results_status` independently describes result availability as None, Partial, or All. Experiment
updates are timestamped progress records. Experiment results contain structured summaries,
metadata, and optional downloadable data packages.

Draft and in-review experiments may be edited. Draft experiments may be submitted for review and
quote preparation. A QuoteSent experiment has associated pricing and expiry information. Accepting
a quote creates an invoice and is a consequential operation requiring operator approval.
